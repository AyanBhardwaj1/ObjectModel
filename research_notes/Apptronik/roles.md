## 3. Recurring themes (counted across the postings above)

1. **C++ on Linux** is near-universal for controls, platform, autonomy and test roles. Interns are asked for *modern C++* fundamentals.
2. **Python** for data, tooling and ML. **PyTorch** (and JAX) appears in every ML, RL and manipulation posting.
3. **Simulation, specifically MuJoCo and Isaac (Lab/Sim/Gym)**, plus **sim-to-real**: system ID, actuator models, domain randomization.
4. **RL for control and locomotion/manipulation**, plus teleop and retargeting data pipelines feeding learning (the "data engine" theme).
5. **ROS 2** (autonomy and navigation) and **behavior trees** (BehaviorTree.CPP).
6. **Real-time and multithreaded software**, and running on hardware ("validate in sim and on physical robots").
7. **Engineering hygiene**: Git, CI/CD, unit and integration tests. In 2026 the intern posting also names **AI coding agents**.

**What intern postings specifically ask for:** C++ and/or Python fundamentals, Git, Linux, and linear algebra/physics for controls. They also want a robotics project (motion, sim, or a real robot), and some want ROS 2, PyTorch, or MuJoCo/Isaac. Older (2025) postings had a 3.0 GPA cutoff and some were MS/PhD-only. The 2026 postings open to BS students. Export-control eligibility is required, and some roles mention lifting 15 lb.

## 4. Recruiting info

- **Timing:** Summer internships run about May/June through summer, in person in Austin. The 2025 posting said start "around May/June 2025". There is also a **12-week fall** ML Ops/ML Systems project. Autonomy intern postings went up around **March 2026**. No Summer 2027 postings had surfaced by Nov 2026. **[inferred]** Expect them in the coming months, so watch the Greenhouse board.
- **Interview (Glassdoor, n=18):** 38.9% of reviews rate the experience positive, difficulty is 2.89/5, and the process averages about 16 days. Stages: 1:1s, phone screens, panels and **presentations**, and occasional skills tests. One SWE report describes a day-long onsite of 5 rounds, mostly technical (https://www.glassdoor.com/Interview/Apptronik-Interview-Questions-E1750713.htm). Interview Query, a non-candidate source, says 5–6 rounds over 3–5 weeks (https://www.interviewquery.com/interview-guides/apptronik-software-engineer).

## 5. Portfolio implications [inferred]

The strongest overlap across intern roles points to a single project: a **C++/Python MuJoCo (or Isaac Lab) humanoid or arm controller**, ideally RL-trained with PyTorch, with sim-to-real-style system ID or domain randomization, ROS 2 integration, and CI tests. For the Developer Experience track, build a **robot-dev tool**: a VS Code extension or CLI that deploys, logs and replays a MuJoCo/ROS 2 sim, built with coding agents. For the ML Ops track, export a policy to ONNX/TensorRT with INT8 quantization and benchmark the latency.
