Reconstruct an object

The full pipeline runs on our servers: parts, joints, physics, URDF and MJCF export, then the MuJoCo checks.

It needs points shaped (T, N, 3) in metres and visible shaped (T, N); labels (N) is optional. No file handy? Download a sample laptop capture.
